# SunoAICreator
Here is a guide and musical knowledge base for use with major Large Language Model (LLM) chatbots—such as Claude (recommended), ChatGPT, Grok, or Gemini—to generate detailed songs. By incorporating specific tags for lyrics, style, and other parameters, you can create high-quality AI-generated songs.

This guide synthesizes all seven of your files into a comprehensive, professional manual designed to eliminate randomness and give you complete control over your musical outputs:
Prompting & Core Foundations: Master the precise 2026 prompt template
 (contrasting generic prompts with descriptive, high-quality alternatives
) and leverage Suno v5.5's native Voice Cloning, Custom Models, and My Taste mechanics
.
Meta Tags & Advanced Parameters: A library of structural, atmospheric, and instrumental tags
 paired with explanations of Weirdness Control, Style Influence, and the Exclude Styles parameter to sculpt your mix and eliminate unwanted elements
.
Vocal Direction & Spatial Placement: Clear definitions of vocal styles (from whisper to grawls/screams)
 and spatial cues ([close mic], [in your ear], or stadium stage reverb)
, structured around the essential rules of Prompt Weight and Tag Moderation
.
12 Expert Production Hacks: Technical formulas for advanced sound design, including concert-style atmospheres
, individual-line emotion acting
, massive SATB choruses
, uppercase dynamic emphasis
, and stable 3-step duet setups
.
Emotional Harmony & Structures: A matrix of emotional chord progressions (for solitude, hope, retro-dreamy lofi vibes, and cinematic tension)
 mapped alongside classic structural templates for Pop, Ballads, EDM, Indie, and Rap
.
Pro-Level Blueprints: Copy-paste-ready styles, prompts, and structures modeled on successful fusions like "Cosmic Echoes" (Psychedelic Rock)
, "Digital Pulse" (EDM)
, and "Cyber Sushi" (Futuristic EDM)
